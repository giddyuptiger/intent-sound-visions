ABOUT:

INTENT SOUND & VISIONS was founded with a singular purpose: to redefine the boundaries of creativity in the realm of sound and visual storytelling.

We recognize a gap in the industry—a gap where traditional boundaries between music, visuals, and production are limiting the potential for truly immersive experiences.

Adjective: resolved or determined to do

SOUND

DEFINITION:

FOUNDED BY:
HNKLLE

Intent Sound & Visions was founded by multi-media creative, HNKLLE. With over a decade of experience mastering the bass, guitar, and piano, HNKLLE's passion for music knows no bounds. As a skilled music director, he leads bands and orchestrates unforgettable performances that captivate audiences.

As an accomplished audio engineer and studio producer, he harnesses the power of sound to shape emotions and tell stories. With a keen sense of arrangement and composition, he breathes life into every project, whether it's in the studio or on stage.

Join us on a journey where sound and vision intertwine, where creativity knows no limits, and where every moment is infused with intention. Welcome to INTENT SOUND & VISIONS—where passion meets purpose, and innovation knows no bounds.

Driven by a passion for collaboration and a desire to empower fellow artists, HNKLLE is committed to helping others bring their creative visions to life. Whether it's through arranging music for live performances or providing guidance in the studio, they approach each project with enthusiasm and expertise.
