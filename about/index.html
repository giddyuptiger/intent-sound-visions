ABOUT:

INTENT SOUND & VISIONS was founded with a singular purpose: to redefine the boundaries of creativity in the realm of sound and visual storytelling.

We recognize a gap in the industry, where traditional boundaries between music, visuals and production limit the potential for truly immersive experiences.

Most events run with one or two of us on site, and up to four for larger shows.

FOUNDED BY:
SPENCER HINKLE

Intent Sound & Visions was founded by multi-media creative Spencer Hinkle, who also performs as HNKLLE. He has played bass, guitar and piano for over a decade, and as a music director he leads bands through live performances.

He is an audio engineer and studio producer, and he works on arrangement and composition as much as on the mix, in the studio and on stage.

Today he designs and runs sound, lighting and video for events across Los Angeles, working with artists, planners and production teams.
